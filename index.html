Who We Are

The Djiboutian American Community Empowerment Project was founded in 2019 to promote the wellbeing of the Djiboutian community in Maine through advocacy, education, mentorship and direct services.

More than two hundred families from Djibouti have made their home in Maine, most of them in Lewiston, Portland and Westbrook. DACEP exists so that those families can face what comes next together rather than alone.

Our Annual Gatherings

Each year the community comes together in Lewiston.

Second Annual Gathering — August 2022

DACEP invited Dr. Abdourahman Barkat God, a public figure in Djibouti, to Lewiston for two days. He spoke with the community about strengthening collaboration and togetherness among Djiboutians, and about the best ways to integrate into their new home in the United States.

First Annual Gathering — September 2021

The first gathering of the Djiboutian community of Maine, held in Lewiston.
